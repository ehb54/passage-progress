Passage Progress: privacy policy

Applies to the Passage Progress browser extension and bookmarklet. Last updated November 11, 2026.

Summary

Passage Progress does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It makes no network requests. Everything it stores stays on your own computer.

What it reads

To show your reading progress, it reads the layout of the page you're viewing (scroll position and where the text sits on screen) inside your browser. It needs permission to run on all websites so the meter can appear on whatever page you're reading. Page contents are never copied or sent anywhere.

What it stores, and where

  • The passage start/end marks and “stopped here” markers you set, saved per page address so they come back when you return. A “stopped here” marker includes the date and time you set it. A mark records a reference to its place in the page and a short snippet (up to 60 characters) of the text at that point, used to find it again.
  • The meter's position on screen, and whether it's shown or hidden.

The extension keeps these in the browser's local extension storage. The bookmarklet keeps them in the website's own local storage on your computer. Nothing is synced to any server. The extension keeps marks for at most the 500 most recently marked pages.

Removing your data

Click Clear on the meter to remove a page's start/end marks, and the × on a “stopped here” marker to delete it. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Bookmarklet data is removed when you clear that site's data in your browser.
